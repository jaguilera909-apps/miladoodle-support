Support

Help with MilaDoodle

Restore Full Version

Open Full Version, complete the purchase check, and select Restore Purchase. Use the store account that originally purchased the Full Version.

Saved artwork

Coloring, tracing, custom pictures, and backgrounds save automatically on your device. Removing the app may remove locally saved artwork.

Stylus support

If stylus input pauses, reconnect the stylus and reopen MilaDoodle. Touch drawing remains available.

Printing

Use the printer button and select an available printer. Printer discovery and printing are handled by your device.

Need more help?

Open a support request with a brief description of the problem. Please do not include a child’s name, artwork, or other personal information.

Privacy Policy

MilaDoodle Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Overview

MilaDoodle is designed for children and families. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, social networking, or tracking technologies.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, contact information, identifiers, usage analytics, location information, photos, or children’s drawings. MilaDoodle does not send artwork or usage data to a developer-operated server.

Information stored on your device

MilaDoodle stores coloring progress, tracing progress, custom drawings, background choices, and a cached purchase-entitlement status locally on the device. This information supports the app’s features and is not received by the developer.

Purchases

The optional Full Version purchase is processed by the app store used to download MilaDoodle. The developer may receive transaction and financial reporting made available by that store, but MilaDoodle does not collect payment-card details.

Printing and external actions

When a grown-up or child chooses to print, MilaDoodle uses the device’s printing interface. The user chooses whether and where to print. Privacy Policy and Support links open in the system browser after the parental gate.

Children’s privacy

MilaDoodle is intended to provide a safe creative experience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no chat features, public profiles, content uploads, or behavioral advertising.

Data deletion

Users can clear individual coloring pages and delete custom pictures inside MilaDoodle. Deleting MilaDoodle from a device removes local app data according to that platform’s behavior. Purchase history is maintained by the app store and can be restored through that store.

Changes to this policy

If MilaDoodle’s data practices change, this policy and the applicable app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changes are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a private-information-free support request. Please do not include a child’s name, artwork, or other personal information.
